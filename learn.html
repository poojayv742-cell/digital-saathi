Basic Digital Literacy Lessons

1. Computer Basics

A computer is an electronic device that processes and stores information.

  • Learn to use a keyboard and mouse.
  • Open, save and organise files.
  • Use applications for study and work.

2. Internet Basics

The internet connects devices and provides access to information and services.

  • Use a browser to visit websites.
  • Search for reliable information.
  • Recognise suspicious links.

3. Email and Communication

Email allows people to exchange messages and documents online.

  • Write a clear email subject.
  • Attach files carefully.
  • Avoid opening suspicious attachments.

4. Online Services

Digital services can help people access education, government information and other resources.

  • Use official websites and apps.
  • Protect personal information.
  • Ask a trusted person for help if needed.
Remember: Never share your password or OTP with anyone who asks for it unexpectedly.
