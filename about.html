About Our Website

Digital Sathi is an educational website designed to promote digital literacy and awareness in the community.

It aims to help people understand digital technology and use online resources safely and effectively in everyday life.

Our Objectives

  • Explain basic computer and internet skills.
  • Promote safe and responsible internet use.
  • Increase awareness of digital payment methods.
  • Help people recognise misleading information.
  • Encourage confidence in using digital services.

Who Can Benefit?

Students, parents, senior citizens, beginners and community members who want to improve their digital knowledge.
