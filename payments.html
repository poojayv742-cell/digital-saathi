What Are Digital Payments?

Digital payments allow money to be transferred electronically without exchanging physical cash.

Examples include UPI, debit cards, credit cards, internet banking and mobile banking.

How to Make a UPI Payment Safely

  1. Open your trusted UPI application.
  2. Select the intended recipient or scan their QR code.
  3. Check the recipient's name and enter the correct amount.
  4. Review the details before confirming.
  5. Enter your UPI PIN only when authorising a payment.
  6. Check the transaction status and keep the receipt.

Important Safety Rules

  • You generally do not need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Entering a UPI PIN authorises a payment; do not enter it just to receive a payment.
  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • Verify unexpected collect requests before approving them.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice an unauthorised transaction.

For official information, visit the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) website.
