Essential Online Safety Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords for your accounts.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
  • Avoid clicking unknown links or downloading suspicious files.
  • Keep your phone, computer and apps updated.
  • Review app permissions and privacy settings.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information on untrusted websites.

Recognising Online Scams

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees, delivery agents, government officials or customer support representatives.

Warning signs include urgent threats, unexpected prizes, requests for money and requests for confidential information.

What should you do if you suspect fraud?

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Contact your bank through its official channels if money or banking information is involved.

In India, report cybercrime at the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. For urgent financial cyber fraud, call 1930.
