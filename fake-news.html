What Is Fake News?

Fake news refers to false or misleading information presented as if it were true news. It may spread through social media, messaging apps, websites and other online platforms.

Warning Signs

  • A shocking headline that pressures you to react immediately.
  • No trustworthy source or author is identified.
  • Images or videos lack context.
  • The information cannot be confirmed by reliable sources.
  • The message asks you to forward it to everyone.

Example: Is This Message Reliable?

Sample message: "Forward this message to ten people today to receive a free smartphone tomorrow!"

What should you do? Do not forward it automatically. Check the offer on the company's official website and look for reliable evidence.

Before Sharing Information

  1. Read beyond the headline.
  2. Check the date and original source.
  3. Compare the claim with reliable sources.
  4. Check images and context carefully.
  5. Do not share claims you cannot verify.
